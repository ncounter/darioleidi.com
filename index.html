Music

I picked up the electric guitar at the age of 15, starting out with a deep dive into '90s punk — Green Day, Sum 41, Offspring, Blink-182, Rancid, and the like. Not long after, I began studying music in a private school, a journey that lasted nearly a decade. During that time, my musical interests gradually shifted toward hard rock and metal — Dream Theater, Angra, Opeth, Alter Bridge, AC/DC, Iron Maiden — while I also immersed myself in shredding techniques inspired by the likes of Kiko Loureiro, Andy Timmons, Joe Satriani, John Petrucci, and Eric Johnson. Throughout my studies, I also played in various local bands just for fun, and with one of them we wrote a few songs and released an album: Qaanaaq – Escape from the Black Iced Forest. Eventually, I took a detour into jazz, which added a whole new layer to my playing. These days, it’s all blended together — I enjoy listening to and playing a bit of everything.
